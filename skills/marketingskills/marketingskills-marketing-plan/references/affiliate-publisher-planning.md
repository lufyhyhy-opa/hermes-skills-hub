# Affiliate-publisher planning

Use this branch when the user explicitly chooses affiliate before holding stock. Treat the requested master plan as a proposed planning artifact, separate from execution approvals.

## Canonical decision pack

Record confirmed model, audience, literal channel selection, total capital period, exact spending amount if selected, team and goal. Keep ambiguous channel groups literal: “Facebook Page/Group or Zalo” does not establish which one exists or that all exist. Save exact selections in brief JSON and an addendum. A budget range is not permission to spend its upper endpoint.

Write one current master, one decision summary, machine-readable economics and a roadmap with every requested day covered. Preserve worker handoffs unchanged but identify the master as the reconciled current interpretation.

## Revenue and evidence recipe

Use this sequence: permitted channel → program-approved affiliate link → merchant checkout → tracked order → valid/approved commission → bank receipt. Merchant GMV is separate from publisher approved revenue. Pending is neither rejection nor cash. Merchant pricing, fulfillment, returns and customer data stay with merchant; the publisher cannot invent guarantees or access.

For each candidate program, save official URL, retrieval date, effective date if stated, exact supporting text and account/campaign limits. Verify the actual channel and publisher type, eligible base, SKU rate, caps, attribution, approval and payout schedule. A network FAQ does not establish campaign approval. An external social link flow does not prove creator in-platform attribution or account eligibility. Preserve conflicts and older document dates. Resolve competitor-program restrictions before running multiple programs; separating channels is not automatically sufficient.

Calculate commission per valid order from the actual net commission ledger. In an explicitly uncapped hypothetical model it can be eligible base × rate. Calculate earnings per tracked order by applying the valid rate once. Break-even **valid** orders = ceiling(matched costs / commission per valid order); do not multiply validity into the denominator again. Keep one cohort and cost scope, approval lag, payout lag, deductions, thresholds and pending visible. Separate contribution, cash result and labor-adjusted result. Unobserved willingness to pay, hours, LTV and payback remain unknown.

## Capital and capacity recipe

Recommend the smallest experiment that can answer a decision. A cap is a maximum, not a target. Test-cell cost sits inside its stage and project total; stage dates never authorize release. Keep any larger capital scenario parked when the model does not support it. Reserve is inside the total and does not fund negative unit economics. Founder labor is not free; measure hours and value separately without double-counting wages already expensed.

Reconcile all specialist handoffs into one role map, one active-SKU limit and one cadence. Distinguish surveyed candidates from active tests, asset variations from complete packages, and placements from independent creatives. Set initial cadence by the whole package including evidence, QA, approval, response and reconciliation; increase only after actual time logs. T0 confirms who owns each role, available minimum hours, channel permissions and one primary program before Day 1; the first week measures and refines that capacity.

## Gates and final checks

Keep content HITL, account readiness and spending approval as separate conditions. For LinTech: verified Gate 1 precedes outline/script production; shotlist leads to the shoot card and WAITING_RAW_VIDEO; humans shoot/upload, accessible Drive raw then passes Gate 2 before editor. Gate 2 is not approval required before humans can shoot. Final package and four QA certificates precede verified Gate 3, then distributor. Missing Telegram/Sheet blocks required execution transitions, not the writing/delivery of a proposed plan. Optional operational notifications do not make mandatory HITL optional.

Run independent strategy, economics/capacity and operations reviews. Reconcile findings rather than copying a reviewer's formula without checking its denominator. Verify roadmap coverage and budget math programmatically, validate citations against saved evidence, keep all unknowns in the decision list and deliver the concrete proposed artifact before asking to approve execution.
