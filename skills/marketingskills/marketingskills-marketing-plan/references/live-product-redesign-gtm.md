# Live-product redesign → executable GTM roadmap

Use for a marketing plan that changes a live product's business model, especially paid entertainment, and includes community funnels, content automation or publishing.

## Contract for the proposed plan

1. **Separate three kinds of truth.** Put confirmed user decisions, proposed design and observed live implementation in distinct fields. A user choosing a redesign is not evidence that the live build changed. Preserve prior plans as inactive baselines; do not inherit their ICP, pricing, metrics or brand when later intake conflicts.
2. **Name a release-readiness prerequisite separately from content approval.** Product/build, legal, payment, privacy and migration evidence gate public traffic or money collection. Conditional plan approval can precede release readiness. Desktop design approval, Telegram plan approval, real raw-footage evidence and final publication approval remain distinct. Do not require the results of a public experiment before approving its plan.
3. **Write a future execution roadmap, not another document calendar.** Each row contains future action, authorization required, owner role, prerequisite evidence, relative day window, real deliverable and acceptance evidence. When implementation is not authorized now, keep the row PROPOSED; still name the actual future build, instrumentation, UAT, content production, approval, publishing and observed pilot result. Reserve “spec/checklist/runbook” for design tasks, not as substitutes for operational outputs.
4. **Put production before acquisition.** Order the dependency chain as plan receipt → outline/copy/script → real raw footage → edit/thumb → required QA → final approval → publication/readback → recruitment → closed-cohort report. If the package requires video, raw-footage review is required. Keep engineering/legal work outside the marketing envelope visible as delivery dependencies rather than treating it as already done.
5. **Declare dependency semantics.** Use finish-to-start unless a named partial milestone is explicit. Validate each numbered dependency exists and finishes before the dependent row begins; report gaps and cycles. Cover the full requested horizon, including days beyond twelve weeks in a 90-day plan. A blocked prerequisite postpones dependent work, not its acceptance standard.
6. **Preserve the requested community-to-sales route.** Show community-led and demo-first proposals side by side, with a voluntary group invitation and direct-play/purchase alternative. Propose one platform, a concrete benefit/ritual, host/mod ownership, opt-in boundary, inbound purchase-intent handoff and checkout path. “Community-first” must name whether it means invitation order or compulsory membership. When the design prohibits a group wall, every experiment arm includes skip/direct access; explicitly supersede any research treatment that required membership.
7. **Use early concept research without pretending it is revenue.** Ask price/right/refusal questions before collecting payment or sending anyone to an unsafe legacy build. Log observed intent as intent, not conversion. If research changes an offer already bound to approval, version and reapprove the affected plan before production.
8. **Connect cash to capacity.** Show monthly cash buckets separately from cohort experiment allocations, founder time, game operations, development, legal and migration obligations. Label vendor estimates and human-hours/package as assumptions. Include capture, edit, graphic composition, QA and publishing tools; native/manual fallbacks are valid. A budget envelope does not prove output capacity or authorize spending.
9. **Keep measurement roles unambiguous.** Select one proposed business North Star; label demo starts as leading indicators and satisfaction/refunds as guardrails. Use one canonical event dictionary, distinguish control-plane schema from product analytics and map shorthand names. Publish rates only for matched closed windows; incomplete cohorts are PENDING and zero denominators UNDEFINED.
10. **Make privacy acceptance operational.** Ban raw PII from marketing logs and UTM, but treat opaque/pseudonymous IDs as protected data. Test withdrawal, suppression, access control and retention/deletion before pilot. Group membership is not permission for personal marketing messages.
11. **Bind desktop decisions without inventing approvals.** Keep a durable decision pack for funnel, angle, price/SKU status, monthly envelope and execution scope. Confirmed choices override older draft recommendations; undecided price nulls out illustrative prices so they cannot be treated as selected offers. Planning-only scope freezes production and spend. A continue instruction may bind an ambiguous near-match id only with an explicit basis, and still leaves Telegram gates waiting until verified receipts exist.
12. **Keep independent verification honest.** Parent-run structural checks on headings, day coverage, dependency finish-to-start, budget sums and ICE scores are required before claiming the plan pack is ready. Do not retry a blocked validator install path, claim historical child self-reports as fresh parent proof, or equate schema/shape checks with production QA, cryptographic approval verification or live UAT.

## Review acceptance cases

- A conditional plan can be reviewed while live release proof remains missing; no public traffic is enabled by that review.
- Recruitment has an actual approved/publication-readback predecessor, not merely a future package specification.
- A community invitation-first arm and demo-first arm both allow skipping the group; the intent-to-treat denominator includes all eligible assignments.
- A 90-day table with only runbooks/specs fails execution acceptance even if every calendar day is covered.
- A monthly envelope and a half-month illustrative cohort do not share a cost denominator by accident.
- Structural checks, source provenance and a saved schema report never stand in for production QA, cryptographic approval verification or live UAT.
- After partial human choices, the active decision pack shows the selected funnel/envelope and keeps undecided price or pending angle fields explicit; production remains frozen under planning-only scope.
- Demo-first as the chosen default still keeps a voluntary community path visible when the original brief asked for community-to-sales, without turning the group into a membership wall.
